Sentiment Analysis Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using our sentiment analyzer

What is a sentiment analyzer and how does it work?

A sentiment analyzer is an artificial intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) tool that classifies the emotional tone of written text. ZendiMind AI evaluates word choice, context, emojis, punctuation intensity, and negated phrases (e.g. "not good") to calculate precise polarity and subjective tone.

Is this AI sentiment analyzer completely free?

Yes, ZendiMind AI is 100% free with no account creation, subscription, or software installation required. You can analyze single sentences, multiple paragraphs, or full files right from your web browser.

Can I analyze customer feedback in Excel, CSV, or PDF documents?

Yes. Select the Upload Spreadsheet or File tab above to upload .xlsx, .xls, .csv, .pdf, .docx, or .txt files. You can choose any text column in your sheet to run bulk sentiment classification, view KPI breakdowns, and export the analyzed data.

What types of content can I analyze with ZendiMind?

ZendiMind is optimized for product reviews (Amazon, App Store, Google Play), customer support tickets, NPS survey responses, employee feedback, social media comments, brand mentions, and business correspondence.
